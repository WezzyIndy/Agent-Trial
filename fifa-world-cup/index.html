The Tournament

A Historic Edition

The 2026 FIFA World Cup marks a landmark moment in football history. For the first time ever, 48 national teams will compete across three host nations — the United States, Canada, and Mexico — making it the largest World Cup in history.

Spanning 39 days from June 11 to July 19, the expanded format introduces 12 groups of four teams each, with the top two from each group plus eight best third-placed sides advancing to a 32-team knockout round.
